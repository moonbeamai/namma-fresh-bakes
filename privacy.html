Privacy

This page explains, in plain words, what does with your details when you order on this website.

What we collect

When you place an order we save:

  • your name and phone number
  • your delivery address (only if you choose delivery)
  • what you ordered, and the total
  • the date and time you asked for
  • any note you wrote

Why we collect it

Only to prepare your order, deliver it or hand it over, and contact you about it. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

The shop owner, and anyone the shop asks to help with orders. The website's database service (Supabase) stores the information for us. If you tap "Send on WhatsApp", your order message also goes through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy rules.

How long we keep it

Finished orders (completed or cancelled) are deleted automatically after about days. Any order, even one that was never finished, is deleted after about days.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your order details at any time. Message us on WhatsApp at and tell us your name and order number.

Your phone and this website

  • Your cart is kept in your own browser so it survives a page refresh. It holds item numbers and quantities only, not your name or address.
  • This site has no advertising trackers.
  • The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see that your device opened the page.
